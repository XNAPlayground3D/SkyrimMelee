# Combat

## Stats

| Stat | Default | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Health | 150 | ~6–8 landed hits |
| Stamina | 100 | +25/s after 0.6 s; no regen while guarding |
| Armor | 0 | 0.12 % reduction per point, cap 80 % (667) |
| Two-handed skill | 60 | Damage ×(1 + 0.4 × skill/100) |
| Block skill | 50 | Block reduction 0.30 + 0.45 × skill/100 |
| Poise | 100 | +25/s after 1 s unhit; empty ⇒ stagger |

Weapon: Iron Greatsword, base damage 20, reach 1.47 m.

## Actions

| Action | Input | Wind-up / Active / Recovery | Stamina | Damage × | Stagger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Slash | LMB | 250 / 220 / 350 | 12 | 1.00 | 10 |
| Power slash (overhead) | Shift+LMB | 600 / 160 / 700 | 30 | 1.80 | 45 |
| Bash | F / LMB while blocking | 160 / 80 / 300 | 15 | 0.40 | 40 |
| Guard raise / hold / lower | hold RMB | 200 / — / 150 | 0 | — | — |

`1` and `2` force left/right arcs for practice. LMB alternates arcs and starts with right-to-left. Shift+LMB is the single power attack: an overhead slam.

- Stamina is charged at wind-up start. Insufficient stamina ⇒ power attack rejected (no state change, no cost); the HUD shows `POWER DENIED`. Light attacks are always allowed and clamp stamina at 0.
- `FighterStats` owns health, stamina, poise, armor, and skills. Stamina regenerates at 25/s after 0.6 s and is frozen while the guard is up; poise regenerates at 25/s after 1 s.
- `LightSlash` from `Idle` alternates the arc; the clip determines the visual, the definition determines the phases.

## Sword motion

One animated transform: `swordWorld = fighterRoot × handRest × Pose(clipTime)`.

```csharp
record struct SwordPose(Vector3 GripOffset, Quaternion Rotation);
record struct SwordKey(float Time, SwordPose Pose, Easing EaseToNext);
record SwordClip(string Name, float Duration, bool Loop, SwordKey[] Keys);
```

Clips are authored with `SwordClips.Pose(translation, rotationDegrees)`: `translation` is the grip position in fighter-local space (next to the fighter), and `rotationDegrees` is Euler X/Y/Z in degrees (pitch/yaw/roll). The helper converts that to a quaternion and subtracts the hand attachment.

Clips: `Idle`, `GuardRaise`, `GuardHold`, `GuardLower`, `SlashLeft`, `SlashRight`, `PowerSlash`, `Bash`, `StaggerMicro`, `StaggerSmall`, `StaggerLarge`, `Death`, `Victory`.

Each attack clip covers its full wind-up + active + recovery and holds the telegraph pose for ≥ 100 ms. The active window is the only time the blade can hit. Poses are sampled at simulation time, so mesh and hitbox are identical. Clips cross-fade over 100 ms when the state changes.

The dev harness: `P` pauses the simulation (the clock time scale becomes 0 and the HUD shows `PAUSE  ON`), `R` resets to idle, `Z` / `X` (or `-` / `=`) changes the sim speed, and `--clip-time=<s>` starts paused at an exact clip time. `4`–`7` preview `StaggerSmall`, `StaggerLarge`, `Death`, and `Victory`. The HUD shows the current clip name, speed multiplier, progress (`50ms/120ms`), and pause status; the F1 overlay shows the clip name, normalized time, and combat state.

The moveset has exactly three attacks: `SlashLeft`, `SlashRight`, and `PowerSlash`. All phase timings, stamina costs, damage multipliers, and stagger values live in `CombatTuning.cs` and mirror the action table above. Each attack holds its telegraph pose for at least 100 ms before the active window; the validator in `AttackDefinitionTests` enforces it.

`SlashLeft` starts and ends in the idle pose: the hand sits at the right hip and the blade stands nearly vertical, matching Skyrim's standing greatsword idle. The clip is a body-orbit cut: the hands orbit a chest-height pivot, and the blade points radially outward, so the hands move tangentially while the tip cuts a wide arc through the opponent's space. The wind-up carries the whole sword right and behind the body — the hands travel ~0.9 m back and ~0.6 m right while the blade stays near-vertical and keeps the idle roll — so the sword translates instead of twisting in the wrist and the tip ends almost behind the fighter's back. It holds there for 100 ms. The blade rolls on the way back so the **cutting edge** leads the sweep, not the flat. The active window is 220 ms so the cut is readable in slow motion; it carries the hands forward and across the chest, the tip drives ~1.0 m forward, and the hilt travels ~1.5 m to the left. The cut finishes low-left and returns to idle.

`SlashRight` is generated by mirroring `SlashLeft` across the fighter's X-Y plane: grip Z and Euler pitch/yaw are negated, while the timing and 110 ms telegraph hold are identical. The dev harness maps `1` / LMB to `SlashLeft` and `2` to `SlashRight`.

`PowerSlash` is the power overhead slam: the hilt rises high above the head with the blade back/up, holds for 100 ms, then drives forward and down with a deep follow-through. The blade rolls so the cutting edge stays in the chop plane; the flat faces sideways. It uses 600 / 160 / 700 ms, costs 30 stamina, requires stamina, and has 1.8× damage / 45 stagger. Shift+LMB plays it.

`Bash` is a short guard shove: the hilt pulls into the chest, holds for 100 ms, then drives the flat forward and returns to the guard. The body stays static; only the sword moves. It uses 160 / 80 / 300 ms, costs 15 stamina, has 0.4× damage and 40 poise, and is only available from `GuardRaise`/`GuardHold`. F or LMB while blocking plays it; if RMB is still held afterwards, `GuardHold` resumes directly at the guard pose, otherwise the sword lowers to idle.

### Guard clips

`GuardRaise` (200 ms) starts at the idle, pulls back slightly, then settles into the guard; `GuardHold` loops for 2 s with ~1.8° of blade breathing; `GuardLower` (150 ms) drops with a small follow-through and settles back to idle. The guard pose puts the hands centered in front of the chest and the blade diagonally across the torso, flat toward the opponent, so the blade sits between the fighters. The dev harness raises on RMB press, holds while RMB is down, and lowers on release.

### Reaction clips

Reaction durations live in `CombatTuning` (`StaggerSmallDuration` 0.60 s, `StaggerLargeDuration` 1.00 s, `DeathDuration` 1.20 s, `VictoryDuration` 1.60 s).

- `StaggerMicro` (250 ms): a tiny timed-block recoil; the blade dips and recovers.
- `StaggerSmall` (600 ms): a light hit snaps the blade ~25° off vertical to the fighter's right and dips the hilt, then the sword settles back to the idle guard.
- `StaggerLarge` (1000 ms): a power hit throws the blade ~45° out, droops it almost horizontal, then hauls it back up to idle.
- `Death` (1200 ms): the blade is knocked up, falls forward and down, and holds with the tip on the floor. The final pose is permanent until a reset.
- `Victory` (1600 ms): the blade rises straight overhead and holds there.

Each reaction pairs its sword clip with a `BodyClip` that applies one rigid transform to the capsule: `StaggerSmall` rocks it 10° to the left, `StaggerLarge` 20°, `Death` topples it 88° about the feet and lifts it by the capsule radius so it rests on the floor, and `Victory` leans it back 12° and holds. There are no joints; the capsule never bends. `4`–`7` in the dev harness play each pair; `R` returns to idle.

### Blade geometry

`BladeGeometry` turns a pose plus hand transform into the world-space grip, blade base, blade tip, and the base/tip radii (local base y = 0.18 m, tip y = 1.47 m, base radius 4.25 cm, tip radius 1.8 cm). The F1 overlay draws the capsule axis and base/tip rings from those values, and the `BladeSweep` uses the same struct. Sampling is shared: no second copy of the blade math. The cut sweeps across the body in the fighter's Z axis, which is the screen plane from the third-person back camera. The blade's broad face is rolled toward the camera on every key so the sweep stays readable. Both hands are part of the sword rig and travel with the hilt; the white primitive body only moves as a rigid reaction pose.

A confirmed contact tints the hit body primitive (head, torso, hips, or legs) for `HitFlashDuration` (300 ms); the same part's `F2` hurtbox outline uses the tint. This is visual feedback only; damage bookkeeping is separate.

| State | Clip | Exits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Idle` | `SlashLeft @ 0` | attack, guard raise, guard hold (bash resume), stagger, dead |
| `Windup` | attack clip | `Active`, `Stagger`, `Dead` |
| `Active` | attack clip | `Recovery`, `Stagger`, `Dead` |
| `Recovery` | attack clip | `Idle`, `Stagger`, `Dead` |
| `GuardRaise` | `GuardRaise` | `GuardHold`, `GuardLower`, attack, `Stagger`, `Dead` |
| `GuardHold` | `GuardHold` | `GuardLower`, attack, `Stagger`, `Dead` |
| `GuardLower` | `GuardLower` | `Idle`, `Stagger`, `Dead` |
| `Stagger` | `StaggerMicro/Small/Large` | `Idle`, `Dead` |
| `Dead` | `Death` | reset only (`Idle`) |

Rules: wind-up/active/recovery can only be interrupted by stagger or death; facing is locked from wind-up to recovery end; only `GuardHold` blocks; guard may attack directly out of `GuardRaise/Hold`; a bash that ends while RMB is held resumes `GuardHold` directly; death beats stagger.

`CombatStateMachine` runs on the fixed 60 Hz step. `TryStartAttack`, `TryStartGuard`, and `TryReleaseGuard` return false when the transition table rejects them, so an attack cannot be cancelled into a guard or another attack (commit lock). `ApplyStagger` and `Kill` interrupt any non-dead state. The F1 overlay shows the state, state time, and the active/block/facing-lock flags.

Blade hitbox: local `(0, 0.18, 0)` → `(0, 1.47, 0)`, transformed to world, approximated by three spheres (r 0.055 / 0.040 / 0.025). See [architecture](architecture.md#physics) for the sweep.

## Damage

```text
raw        = 20 × damageMultiplier × (1 + 0.4 × skill / 100)
armored    = raw × (1 − min(0.80, armor × 0.0012))
blocked    = armored × (1 − blockReduction)        // 0.30 + 0.45 × blockSkill/100
timed      = armored × (1 − min(0.85, blockReduction + 0.15))
guardBreak = armored
```

Rounded once at the end (`AwayFromZero`), minimum 1 on any connecting hit. Example at skill 60 (`raw 24.8`):

| Situation (armor 300, skill 60) | Damage |
| --- | --- |
| Clean hit | 16 |
| Blocked (block skill 50) | 8 |
| Timed block (block skill 50) | 5 |
| Guard break | 16 |
| Power slash clean | 29 |

`DamageModel.Compute` is the single implementation of the formula. It returns a `DamageBreakdown` (kind, raw, armored, final, reduction %, stamina cost); block stamina is `0.6 × armored`, timed block `0.3 × armored`. Damage rounds once with `AwayFromZero`, with a minimum of 1 on any connecting hit.

Each attack owns a `SwingHitSet`. The first contact with a hurtbox produces a `HitEvent` (attacker, target, hurtbox kind, world point, normal, attack definition, active-window progress); later contacts with that same hurtbox in the same swing are ignored. The next attack starts with a clean set. A confirmed hit tints the hit primitive for `HitFlashDuration` (300 ms). Damage is applied separately.

## Blocking

A hit is blocked only if the defender is in `GuardHold`, the guard shape is registered, contact is with the guard blade, and the attacker is inside the front 120° cone. `GuardResolver` compares the earliest guard contact with the earliest body contact; the guard wins a tie within 1e-4 s, otherwise the first contact decides. A guard held for less than `GuardTimedBlockWindow` (250 ms) is a timed block; older is a normal block. The 100 ms lower bound in the table is a T5 balance target.

| Timing / stamina | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Guard held > 250 ms | Block: reduced damage, stamina cost `0.6 × armored` |
| Guard raised 100–250 ms before impact | Timed block: stronger reduction, half stamina, attacker gets 250 ms `StaggerMicro` |
| Block cost > remaining stamina | Guard break: full damage, guard removed, 1000 ms stagger |
| From outside the cone, or while raising/lowering | Clean hit |

Stamina drain on a blocked hit is paid by the defender. A timed block is the reward for reading, not reacting.

`DefenseResolver` applies the same incoming attack to a defender: a block or timed block spends its stamina cost (0.6× / 0.3× armored). If the cost exceeds the defender's remaining stamina, the guard breaks instead: full armored damage, stamina drained to 0, guard force-lowered, and a 1000 ms stagger. A successful timed block also gives the attacker a 250 ms `StaggerMicro`; the guard cannot be re-raised while staggered.

## Poise and stagger

Every hit subtracts `Stagger`, and poise is restored at 100 on entering `Stagger`. `StaggerRules.Resolve` applies the attack's poise damage: light hits stagger only when poise reaches zero, power attacks always stagger, and a staggered fighter cannot attack or guard until the timer expires.

| Source | Duration | Clip |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Poise emptied (light hit) | 600 ms | `StaggerSmall` |
| Power attack clean hit, or poise emptied by one | 1000 ms | `StaggerLarge` |
| Timed-block recoil | 250 ms | `StaggerMicro` |
| Guard break | 1000 ms | `StaggerLarge` |
| Death | 1200 ms, terminal | `Death` |

Stagger cancels any action and blocks all input, guard included. Damage can still be taken.

## Edge cases

| Case | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Both land on the same tick | Both resolve; trades are legal |
| Double KO | Round draw: no score, replay the round |
| Guard and body at equal time-of-impact | Block wins |
| Guard contact first, body later in the same swing | Swing consumed by the block |
| Same hurtbox twice in one swing | Ignored (per-swing hit set) |
| Attack connects on the last active tick | Resolves before entering recovery |
| Attacker dies mid-swing | Attack stops, no further hits |
| Stamina exactly equals cost | Allowed |

## Rounds and input

- Best of three, 5 s intro, 3 s post-death hold, full reset between rounds.
- `DuelDirector` owns the round, score, phase, and match winner. A death scores the round and starts the 3 s hold; the first to 2 wins. A double KO replays the round without score. On a new round the caller resets stats, FSMs, clips, hit sets, and guard shapes.
- Attack intents buffer for 120 ms of real time; one pending intent, newest wins; it fires on the first legal frame and rejected intents (stamina, no guard) are consumed. Guard is level-triggered, so holding RMB through recovery raises on the first legal frame.
- `InputRouter` owns the buffer. `MyraHost.CapturesInput` pauses gameplay input while a modal or keyboard-focused widget is open. The F1 overlay shows the buffered intent and the last rejection reason.
- Guard is level-triggered, never buffered. Input is snapshotted once per simulation tick; UI captures first.

All values live in `Combat/CombatTuning.cs`. Changing one requires updating the tables above in the same commit.
